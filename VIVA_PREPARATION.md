# Viva Preparation

Use these prompts to check understanding. Answer in your own words and relate
answers to the implementation and actual experimental evidence.

1. What is a neural network?
2. What is a weight? What is a bias?
3. What is a loss function?
4. Why is cross-entropy loss used for classification?
5. What is gradient descent?
6. What is the learning rate?
7. What is an epoch?
8. What are forward propagation and backpropagation?
9. Why are gradients cleared with `optimizer.zero_grad()`?
10. How do SGD and Adam differ?
11. What does momentum do?
12. What is weight initialization?
13. How do Xavier and He initialization differ?
14. What is a loss landscape?
15. What is a local minimum? What is a global minimum?
16. What is a saddle point?
17. What is the difference between a flat and sharp region?
18. What does a 2D loss-landscape slice represent?
19. Why can models with similar test accuracy have different training losses
    or loss landscapes?
20. What is overfitting?
21. Why is a separate test dataset kept?
22. What is reproducibility, and how is it supported here?
23. What limitations apply to this Two Moons experiment and its landscape
    plots?
